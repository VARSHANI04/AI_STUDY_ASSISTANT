# AI Study Assistant

## 1. Problem Statement

Students often have to study large amounts of educational content within a limited amount of time. Reading lengthy study materials, identifying important points,
preparing practice questions, and checking answers manually can be time-consuming.

The **AI Study Assistant** aims to provide an intelligent learning tool that processes study materials and helps students understand and practice the content more
efficiently.

## 2. Scope

The scope of the project includes:

* Accepting study material as text input.
* Preprocessing the provided study material.
* Generating concise summaries.
* Extracting important key points.
* Generating practice questions.
* Evaluating student answers.
* Answering questions based on the provided study material.
* Generating a basic study report.

The current project is developed as a prototype using **Python and Google Colab**.

## 3. Target Users

The primary target users are:

* College and university students.
* Students preparing for examinations.
* Learners studying from lengthy educational materials.
* Students who want quick summaries and practice questions.

## 4. High-Level Features

### Study Material Processing

Allows users to provide study material and processes the text for further analysis.

### Automatic Summarization

Generates a concise summary from the provided study material using an NLP-based summarization model.

### Key Point Extraction

Identifies important sentences and key information from the study material.

### Practice Question Generation

Generates questions from the provided learning content for practice.

### Answer Evaluation

Evaluates student answers and provides a quiz score.

### Question Answering

Allows students to ask questions related to their study material and retrieves relevant information from the content.

### Study Report

Provides a summary of the study session, including content statistics and quiz performance.

## 5. Technologies Used

* Python
* Google Colab
* Natural Language Processing (NLP)
* NLTK
* Scikit-learn
* Hugging Face Transformers
* DistilBART
* GitHub

## 6. Expected Outcome

The project is expected to provide students with a simple AI-based study assistant that can transform study material into summaries, key points, practice questions,
evaluated quizzes, and relevant answers.
